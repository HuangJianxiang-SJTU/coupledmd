CoupledMD Supplementary Data

S1 is the included-system inventory; S2 combines 13 unresolved and two excluded records.
S3 describes every field actually serialized in S1-S10. S4 is source-inventory evidence,
S5 contains detected pockets plus two explicit valid zero-pocket records, and S6 contains
tidy system/interface/metric gateway summaries. S7 is a selected-replica ledger, S8 is a
readiness/QC summary with evidence pointers, and S9 measures current portal/API coverage.
This package does not claim a frozen archive, DOI, archive checksum manifest, complete
621-file trajectory manifest, or self-contained per-file QC deposit.
